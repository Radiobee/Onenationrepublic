❖ Mandatory Stripe Requirements

Why Partner Accounts Get Stuck on "Restricted" (And How to Turn Yours Green)

Simply creating a login password from our Stripe invite link does not activate your account. Stripe locks every new connected account as Restricted until you complete all identity, tax, and bank payout requirements. Stripe's system will not allow us to generate your 50/50 split links while your account says Restricted.

Restricted

Cannot Generate Split Links

Missing ID scan, SSN/EIN, website field, or payout bank account. Transfers capability is locked—we cannot attach your account to any payment links yet.

Enabled

Ready for 50/50 Partner Hub!

All identity, tax, and bank payout checks are verified. Both Merchant & Recipient (Transfers) capabilities are active and ready for instant link generation.

❖ 5-Point Stripe Onboarding Cheat Sheet (Complete All 5 Steps)

  • 1. Business Type Selection: Select "Individual / Sole Proprietor" for the fastest instant verification. (Warning: Only select "Company / LLC" or "Trust" if you have your official IRS EIN Confirmation Letter and formation paperwork ready to upload immediately—otherwise Stripe will lock your account in Restricted status!)
  • 2. What to Enter for "Business Website": Do not leave this blank or exit the page! When Stripe asks for your Business Website URL, paste https://onenationrepublic.com (or click "Add a product description instead" and type: "Independent educational software and study archive promotion.")
  • 3. Personal Identity & Tax Info: Enter your full legal name, date of birth, physical street address (Stripe does not allow P.O. Boxes for identity verification), and your SSN (or EIN). If Stripe prompts you to scan your Driver's License or ID with your phone camera, complete that scan immediately.
  • 4. Attach a Payout Checking Account: Enter a valid checking account Routing Number and Account Number (or eligible payout debit card). Stripe keeps your Transfers capability locked until a payout bank is attached.
  • 5. Click "Agree & Submit" & Clear All Top Banners: After submitting, log into dashboard.stripe.com and check the top of your screen. If you see an orange or red "Information required" or "Complete your profile" banner, click it and finish the remaining prompts until all warnings disappear.
🚫 DO NOT CLICK THE INVITE LINK TWICE (NO DUPLICATE ACCOUNTS): If you stop halfway through onboarding and need to finish later, do not click the original invite link again—doing so creates a second blank Restricted account under your email. Instead, go directly to dashboard.stripe.com, sign in with the email and password you already created, and click the "Complete your profile / Information required" banner at the top of your dashboard.
