❖ Technical Protocol

4-Step Partner Onboarding & Stripe Verification

Important Technical Note: Stripe cannot split payments when traffic is sent to someone else's Linktree or general checkout link. To receive automated 50/50 payouts, your traffic must use your unique Stripe Connect split links hosted on the standardized One Nation Partner Directory.

  • Step 1: Accept Your Stripe Connect Invitation

    We will send an official Stripe Connect invitation to your email. Click the link to create or link your Stripe account to One Nation Republic LLC.

  • Step 2: Complete Identity & Bank Verification (Unlock "Transfers")

    Inside your Stripe dashboard, click the "Information required / Complete your profile" banner. Submit your ID verification, SSN/EIN, and payout checking account so your account status switches from Restricted to Enabled with both Merchant and Transfers capabilities active.

  • Step 3: Receive Your Standardized One Nation Directory Link

    Once your Stripe account shows Active / Transfers Enabled, we generate your three 50/50 split payment links (Lawful Library, ONRAP, and IDAOS) and plug them into our standardized One Nation Partner Directory template (e.g., onenationrepublic.com/links-miller.html).

  • Step 4: Share Your Directory Link

    Place your standardized One Nation Partner Directory link in your bio or share it directly with your audience. Every sale made through your split links pays your 50% share automatically.
