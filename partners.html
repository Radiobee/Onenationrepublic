❖ Technical Protocol

4-Step Partner Onboarding & Stripe Verification

Important Technical Note: Stripe cannot split payments when traffic is sent to someone else's Linktree or checkout link. To receive automated 50/50 payouts, you must promote your own dedicated Partner Hub URL containing your unique Stripe split links.

  • Step 1: Accept Your Stripe Connect Invitation

    We will send an official Stripe Connect invitation to your email. Click the link to create or link your Stripe account to One Nation Republic LLC.

  • Step 2: Complete Identity & Bank Verification (Unlock "Transfers")

    Inside your Stripe dashboard, click the "Information required / Complete your profile" banner. Submit your ID verification, SSN/EIN, and payout checking account so your account status switches from Restricted to Enabled with both Merchant and Transfers capabilities active.

  • Step 3: Dedicated Partner Portal Deployment

    Once your Stripe account shows Active / Transfers Enabled, we generate your three 50/50 split payment links (IDAOS, Lawful Library, and ONRAP) and deploy your dedicated hub page (e.g., onenationrepublic.com/links-yourname.html).

  • Step 4: Place Your Hub Link in Your Bio

    Put your new One Nation Partner Hub URL at the top of your TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or Linktree bio. Every sale made through your page pays your 50% split automatically.
